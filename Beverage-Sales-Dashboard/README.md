# Beverage Sales Dashboard Analysis

## Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing beverage sales performance across regions, beverage brands, months and quarters. The project demonstrates how sales data can be transformed into useful insights for business decision-making.

## Objectives

* Monitor total revenue and units sold.
* Compare sales volume across regions.
* Analyse monthly revenue trends.
* Evaluate quarterly performance.
* Compare average price per unit.
* Support data-driven sales and marketing decisions.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Data visualisation

## Key Performance Indicators

* **Total Revenue:** 8M
* **Total Units Sold:** 16M
* **Average Price per Unit:** 0.48

## Key Findings

* **West** recorded the highest regional sales volume, at approximately 3.94M units.
* **Midwest** recorded the lowest regional sales volume among the displayed regions, at approximately 2.78M units.
* **December** had the highest visible monthly revenue, at approximately 0.98M.
* **July** followed closely, at approximately 0.97M.
* **Q3** accounted for the largest displayed share of quarterly revenue, at 30.59%.
* **Q1** recorded the smallest displayed share, at 17.11%.

## Business Recommendations

1. Investigate the factors contributing to strong sales in the West region.
2. Identify opportunities to improve performance in lower-volume regions.
3. Plan inventory and marketing activities around high-performing months.
4. Investigate the factors behind weaker Q1 performance.
5. Use brand and regional filters to support more targeted sales decisions.

## Project Outcome

This project demonstrates practical skills in Power BI dashboard development, KPI reporting, data visualisation and business insight generation.
